# KPIs and Key Metrics for Analysis

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*Primary measures of business performance*

### Core KPIs
- **Total Sales**
- **Total Profit**
- **Profit Margin (%)**

---

## 2. Key Metrics (Drivers of KPIs)
*Metrics that explain changes in KPIs*

### Pricing & Revenue Metrics
- **Discount (%)**
- **Average Selling Price** (Sales ÷ Quantity)
- **Quantity Sold**

---

### Product Performance Metrics
- **Category**
- **Sub-Category**

---

### Geographic Performance Metrics
- **Region**
- **State**

---

### Operational Efficiency Metrics
- **Order Processing Days**
- **Ship Mode**

---

### Time-Based Metrics (Trend Analysis)
- **Order Month / Quarter / Year**
- **Shipped Month / Quarter / Year**

---

## 3. Supporting Metrics
*Used for filtering and segmentation*

- **Segment**
- **Postal Code**
- **Row ID**

---

## 4. KPI to Metric Mapping

| KPI | Key Metrics to Analyze |
|-----|------------------------|
| **Sales** | Quantity, Discount, Category, Region, Time |
| **Profit** | Discount, Sales, Quantity, Sub-Category, Region |
| **Profit Margin** | Discount, Category, Sub-Category, Average Selling Price |

---

## 5. Dashboard Focus (Recommended)

### KPI Section
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %

### Analysis Sections
- Profit Margin by Sub-Category
- Discount vs Profit
- Sales vs Quantity
- Profit by Region and State
- Monthly and Quarterly Sales & Profit Trends
